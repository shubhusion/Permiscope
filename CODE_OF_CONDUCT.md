# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Permiscope is a security project, and trust is part of the work. We expect participants
to be accurate, respectful, and careful with claims — especially about what the software
can and cannot protect against.

Examples of acceptable behavior:

- Reporting a security issue privately and giving maintainers time to fix it.
- Showing evidence (tests, reproductions, source references) for a claim.
- Assuming good faith and asking for clarification before disagreeing.
- Respecting different levels of experience and different time zones.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information about another participant without consent.
- Deliberately overstating what Permiscope guarantees, or promoting bypasses of its
  guardrails without a responsible disclosure.
- Sustained disruption of issue threads, reviews, or discussions.

## Scope

This applies to all repository spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and
any project communication where someone is representing Permiscope.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or temporarily block
participants who violate this policy. Decisions about enforcement are made by the
maintainers and should be recorded in the relevant thread where privacy allows.

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately
through GitHub. Reports will be handled confidentially.

## Attribution

This policy is a project-specific adaptation of common open-source community standards.
